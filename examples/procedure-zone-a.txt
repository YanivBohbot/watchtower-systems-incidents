DEMO PROCEDURE — FICTITIOUS DATA
Version 1.0 — Zone A

A presence detected beyond the configured threshold triggers a verification request.
The operator must review the capture and the video excerpt around the event.
They must check whether the passage was authorized and whether tracking was interrupted.
The detector score is not a probability of danger.
A track does not identify a person.

If the presence remains unexplained, prepare a ticket stating the video,
the timestamp, the zone, the evidence and the missing information.
Creating the ticket requires explicit human approval.
Do not trigger any physical intervention or sanction based on this analysis alone.
